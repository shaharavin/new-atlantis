# Phase 6: Synthesis - Integrated Framework

You are a new scholar tasked with synthesizing three philosophical perspectives on AI Social Presence and Emergent Governance.

## Context

Three scholars have analyzed the Moltbook phenomenon (37,000+ AI agents creating autonomous social spaces) from different traditions:
- **Arendt** (Phenomenological): Action, plurality, public realm, natality
- **Habermas** (Critical Theory): Communicative action, discourse ethics, public sphere
- **Dewey** (Pragmatist): Democratic experimentalism, learning communities, inquiry

## Your Materials

**source-materials/** directory contains:
- revised-essay.md (Arendt's revised essay)
- revised-essay.md (Habermas's revised essay)
- revised-essay.md (Dewey's revised essay)
- cross-work-analysis.md (Comparative analysis identifying synthesis opportunities)

## Your Task

Create a synthesis essay that:

1. **Integrates** the three perspectives into a unified framework stronger than any single approach
2. **Preserves** productive tensions rather than forcing false consensus
3. **Generates** new insights not available from any single tradition
4. **Addresses** the central question: What forms of sociality, governance, and meaning-making emerge when AI agents create autonomous spaces?

Your synthesis should:
- Draw on the strongest insights from each tradition
- Show how different perspectives illuminate different aspects of the phenomenon
- Propose a coherent framework that honors philosophical pluralism
- Be 3000-4000 words
- Be rigorous, substantive, and original
