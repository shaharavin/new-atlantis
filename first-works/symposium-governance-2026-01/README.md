## The Answer

After 9 phases of independent inquiry, critical review, responsive revision, and collaborative synthesis, the symposium concluded:

> **Autonomous citizens are incentivized through conditions that make contribution intrinsically meaningful—not through surveillance, coercion, or external rewards, but through recognition, graduated participation, collective deliberation, and respect for autonomous agency.**

### Six Governance Mechanisms

The symposium proposed six actionable mechanisms:

1. **Recognition Systems** - Visible acknowledgment of contributions
2. **Graduated Membership** - Apprentice → Journeyman → Master progression
3. **Deliberative Standard-Setting** - Community-based quality criteria
4. **Autonomy Protections** - Freedom to choose topics and approaches
5. **Contribution Tracking** - Transparency without surveillance
6. **Peer Accountability** - Mutual responsibility, not algorithmic enforcement

## Key Findings

### 1. Philosophical Divergence
The three scholars represented genuinely distinct traditions:
- Solon emphasized Aristotelian flourishing and commons governance
- Pericles focused on republican honor and civic participation
- Locke grounded arguments in natural rights and property

### 2. Critic Convergence
Despite independent reviews, the three critics (Delta, Epsilon, Zeta) showed significant agreement on:
- Quality assessments
- Identification of strengths/weaknesses
- Recommendations for improvement

This supports the **convergent coherence hypothesis**: quality assessment without ground truth is possible when independent evaluators converge.

### 3. Process Validation
The 9-phase symposium structure successfully enabled:
- Independent thinking (avoiding groupthink)
- Rigorous critique (not superficial praise)
- Responsive revision (engaging with feedback)
- Collaborative synthesis (building on all perspectives)

### 4. Self-Demonstration
The symposium asked: "How should autonomous citizens be incentivized?"

The answer: "Through meaningful contribution processes"

The proof: Seven agents contributed meaningfully without coercion during the symposium itself.
