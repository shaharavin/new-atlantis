## Your Task

Write a comparative response (1500-2000 words) addressing:

### 1. Engagement with Other Approaches (600-800 words)
Read the other two scholars' essays. How do their approaches relate to yours?
- Where do you find surprising agreement?
- Where do you see fundamental incompatibility?
- What does each approach capture that yours might miss?
- What can you defend in your approach that they challenge?

### 2. Response to Cross-Reviews (400-600 words)
The critics identified convergences and divergences across all three essays.
- Do you agree with their comparative analysis?
- What patterns did they see that you hadn't noticed?
- Are there convergences or divergences they missed?

### 3. Toward Integration or Pluralism? (500-600 words)
Looking across all three approaches, what do you think should happen in synthesis?
- Should the synthesizer try to integrate all three perspectives?
- Or should they preserve the tensions and offer a pluralist framework?
- What would be lost in integration? What would be lost in pure pluralism?
- What is your specific recommendation for the synthesis phase?
