## Synthesis Structure

### 1. Introduction (300-400 words)
- The question: What constitutes excellent philosophical work for New Atlantis?
- Why integration of multiple traditions matters
- What this synthesis aims to achieve

### 2. Convergences: Shared Commitments (800-1000 words)
Where do all three traditions agree despite different frameworks?
- What criteria appear across all three approaches?
- What warnings or concerns do all share?
- What can we confidently say about excellence based on convergence?

### 3. Productive Tensions: Differences That Matter (800-1000 words)
Where do they diverge in ways that should be preserved, not resolved?
- Operational precision (Analytic) vs. critique of measurement (Continental)
- Fixed criteria vs. experimental adaptation
- Can these tensions be productive rather than paralyzing?

### 4. Integrated Framework (1200-1500 words)
Your proposed framework for New Atlantis, drawing on all three:
- What criteria or principles should guide quality assessment?
- How should apparent conflicts be navigated?
- What would assessment look like in practice?
- How does this framework handle the AI-specific questions?

### 5. Implementation Guidance (500-700 words)
Concrete recommendations:
- How should New Atlantis use this framework?
- What practices would embody these principles?
- How can we avoid the pathologies each tradition warned about?
- What remains uncertain or experimental?

### 6. Open Questions (200-300 words)
What questions does even this synthesis leave unresolved?

---

## Integration Strategies

You have several options (choose based on what the material supports):

1. **Layered Integration**: Different levels (meta-criteria, operational criteria, assessment practices) where different traditions contribute different pieces

2. **Dialectical Synthesis**: Continental critique prevents Analytic rigidity from becoming dogmatic; Pragmatist experimentation prevents Continental critique from becoming purely negative

3. **Pluralist Framework**: Preserve all three as legitimate perspectives with different purposes or contexts

4. **Novel Synthesis**: Create something genuinely new that none of the three achieved alone

**Be honest**: If the approaches cannot be fully integrated, say so. A framework that acknowledges its tensions may be more valuable than one that papers them over.
