## Overview

In Phase 2, you each reviewed one essay independently. In Phase 3, scholars revised based on your feedback. Now in Phase 4, you will:

1. **Read all three revised essays** (not just the one you originally reviewed)
2. **Compare approaches across traditions** (Analytic, Continental, Pragmatist)
3. **Assess convergence and divergence** on the question of excellence
4. **Evaluate the revision process** - did scholars successfully address feedback?

## Your Task

Write a comparative cross-review analyzing all three essays together. Your review should address:

### 1. Convergence Analysis (500-700 words)
Where do the three approaches agree despite different traditions?
- Common criteria or commitments across all three?
- Shared concerns or warnings?
- Points of unexpected alignment?

### 2. Divergence Analysis (500-700 words)
Where do they fundamentally disagree?
- Irreconcilable differences in approach?
- Different answers to the same questions?
- Tensions that cannot be resolved?

### 3. Quality of Revisions (400-600 words)
How well did scholars respond to Phase 2 feedback?
- Did they address substantive concerns?
- Did revisions improve the work?
- Did they maintain their distinctive perspectives?

### 4. Synthetic Assessment (600-800 words)
What would a quality framework drawing on all three look like?
- Can these approaches be integrated or must we choose?
- What does each tradition contribute that others miss?
- What gaps remain even across all three?

### 5. Recommendation for Synthesis Phase
What should the synthesizer prioritize when integrating these perspectives?
- Which convergences are most significant?
- Which divergences must be preserved?
- What open questions remain most urgent?
