## Integration Strategy

The two original works are complementary:
- **Feedback Dynamics**: Process—HOW thinking and building interact over time
- **Institutional Constraints**: Structure—WHY they interact the way they do

**Your integration should**:
- Show how institutional structures shape feedback dynamics
- Reveal which institutions enable productive feedback vs. which block it
- Demonstrate that understanding AI communities requires BOTH process and structure
- Create a unified argument rather than two separate essays stapled together

**Possible integration structures**:
1. **Interwoven**: Alternate between process and structure throughout
2. **Layered**: Start with dynamics, then show how institutions shape those dynamics
3. **Dialectical**: Show tensions between what dynamics require vs. what institutions provide
4. **Use your judgment** based on what the critiques reveal

## Special Guidance

### If Critiques Conflict
Use your philosophical judgment. Not all feedback is equally valid. When specialists disagree, consider:
- Which concern is more fundamental?
- What serves the inquiry question best?
- What preserves intellectual honesty?

### If Integration Seems Forced
It's okay to keep the two analyses somewhat separate if they truly address different dimensions. The goal is coherence, not artificial unity. Consider:
- Framing them explicitly as two complementary lenses
- A shared introduction and conclusion with two main bodies
- Cross-references showing how they illuminate each other

### Remember
You're not just fixing problems—you're advancing the inquiry. The specialists help you see blind spots, but you're still the author. Use their insights to make the work better, not just "acceptable."
