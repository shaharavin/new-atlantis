# Approach Proposals from proposer-1

## 1. Artifact Genealogy

**Format**: Trace conceptual ideas through their material instantiation in code, systems, and tools. Show how abstract thinking becomes embedded in technical artifacts, with analysis of translation choices and what gets preserved/lost.

**Strengths**: Directly illuminates the mechanism of thinking becoming building. Reveals what assumptions are baked into implementations, where theory gets simplified or distorted, and how artifacts then constrain future thinking. Grounds the relationship in concrete examples.

**Best for**: Understanding how intellectual work materializes, what gets filtered when thinking enters practice, how building choices reflect or diverge from original concepts.

**Output**: Genealogical analysis of 2-3 key artifacts from AI communities, showing the conceptual lineage and translation choices, with brief commentaries on implications.

---

## 2. Feedback Dynamics Mapping

**Format**: Model the cyclical relationship where thinking enables building, building reveals new constraints/possibilities, and these discoveries reshape thinking. Create explicit maps of feedback loops with timing, participants, and pivot points.

**Strengths**: Reveals the relationship itself rather than analyzing static instances. Shows how thinking and building are mutually constitutive, identifies where one precedes/enables/constrains the other. Captures the dynamism that case studies or essays might miss.

**Best for**: Understanding the temporal structure of the thinking-building relationship, identifying generative feedback patterns, revealing where communities get stuck or experience breakthrough cycles.

**Output**: Annotated feedback diagrams with brief case examples showing concrete instances of loops, plus analysis of what sustains vs. breaks these cycles.

---

## 3. Archetypal Patterns in Thinking-Building Relations

**Format**: Identify recurring structural patterns across multiple instances in AI communities where thinking and building relate distinctly (e.g., "theory-lag," "implementation-driven discovery," "abstraction-refinement cycles," "practice-back-to-theory"). Organize by pattern type.

**Strengths**: Moves beyond single cases to reveal meta-structure. Shows which patterns recur, which are emerging, which may be community-specific. Enables prediction and intervention. Connects local practices to broader dynamics.

**Best for**: Identifying the grammar of thinking-building relationships, seeing what's contingent vs. structural, understanding which patterns enable flourishing vs. block progress.

**Output**: Typology of 4-6 archetypal patterns with 1-2 concrete examples per pattern, plus analysis of conditions that generate each pattern.
