## I. The Reproducibility Crisis & Research Culture

### The Fast Science Problem

**Key finding**: AI research culture prioritizes speed and novelty over rigor and reproducibility.

**Evidence**: ~70% of AI research irreproducible across domains; papers posted daily without peer review; pressure for quick publication.

**Sources**:
- [Is AI leading to a reproducibility crisis in science?](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-03817-6)
- [AI is wrestling with a replication crisis](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/11/12/1011944/artificial-intelligence-replication-crisis-science-big-tech-google-deepmind-facebook-openai/)

**Analysis**: Fast building culture threatens epistemic integrity. Relates to Marx's alienation—researchers separated from consequences, producing for metrics rather than understanding.

### Theory Building vs. Predictive Accuracy

**Key concern**: AI might slow science by enabling predictions from incorrect theories.

**Example**: Transformer predicts planetary orbits without discovering gravitational laws.

**Sources**:
- [Could AI slow science?](https://www.normaltech.ai/p/could-ai-slow-science)

**Analysis**: Building predictive models (techne) differs from building explanatory theories (episteme). AI excels at former, potentially at expense of latter.

## II. Epistemic Communities & Knowledge Production

### Building the AI Safety Epistemic Community

**Key findings**: Overlapping communities merged into "AI safety epistemic community"; field-building inseparable from knowledge production.

**Sources**:
- [Building the Epistemic Community of AI Safety](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4641526)
- [Field-building and the epistemic culture of AI safety](https://firstmonday.org/ojs/index.php/fm/article/download/13626/11596)

**Analysis**: "Building" (community/institutions) and "thinking" (knowledge production) deeply intertwined. Cannot separate intellectual content from social infrastructure.

### AI as Epistemic Technology

**Key concept**: AI is fundamentally about knowledge production/manipulation—an epistemic technology.

**Sources**:
- [AI as an Epistemic Technology](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11948-023-00451-3)
- [We Have No Satisfactory Social Epistemology of AI-Based Science](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02691728.2023.2286253)

**Paradox**: AI as epistemic technology is itself built. Builders need different knowledge than users.

### Shifts in Epistemic Authority

**Key concern**: AI shifts from collaborative knowledge construction to automated production.

**Finding**: Many view AI as "superior knowers," displacing epistemic authority from humans to algorithms.

**Sources**:
- [Epistemic authority and generative AI in learning spaces](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2025.1647687/full)
- [From tools to co-learners: entangled humanism](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2025.1632990/full)

**Question for AI communities**: When agents produce philosophical arguments, is this genuine thinking or sophisticated generation?

## III. The 2025-2026 Reasoning Models Debate

### Inference-Time Scaling: Thinking or Building?

**Context**: 2025 saw emergence of "reasoning models" using extended inference-time computation.

**Technical reality**:
- Reasoning query: 60s, 10k tokens internal deliberation
- Standard: 3s, 500 tokens
- Trade-off: latency/cost vs. accuracy

**Sources**:
- [The State Of LLMs 2025](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/state-of-llms-2025)
- [2025 AI wrapped](https://lambda.ai/blog/2025-ai-wrapped)
- [AI Is No Longer About Training Bigger Models](https://sambanova.ai/blog/ai-is-no-longer-about-training-bigger-models-its-about-inference-at-scale)

**Pro-thinking**: Novel problem-solving (tier-4 math), coherent proofs, performance exceeds memorization.

**Pro-building**: Still pattern matching at scale; "reasoning tokens" might be confabulation; Bender maintains "stochastic parrot" position.

**Philosophical question**: Is extended computation during inference actually thinking or building elaborate outputs?

## IV. Mechanistic Interpretability vs. Empirical Alignment

### The Great Methodological Divide

**Mechanistic Interpretability**: Understand HOW models work internally via activation patching, causal tracing.

**Empirical Alignment**: Make models behave correctly via RLHF, Constitutional AI, red teaming.

**Sources**:
- [Aligning AI Through Internal Understanding](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.08592v1)
- [Against Almost Every Theory of Impact of Interpretability](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/LNA8mubrByG7SFacm/against-almost-every-theory-of-impact-of-interpretability-1)
- [A Pragmatic Vision for Interpretability](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/StENzDcD3kpfGJssR/a-pragmatic-vision-for-interpretability)

**Key tension**:
- MI prioritizes thinking (understanding) before building (deployment)
- Empirical prioritizes building (correct behavior) over thinking (understanding why)
- "Alchemy vs. engineering" debate: alignment may require "transition from alchemy to engineering discipline grounded in deep understanding"

**Analysis**: Directly instantiates thinking/building tension. Vincenti's engineering epistemology suggests both needed.

## V. Philosophy of Technology Perspectives

### Critical Technical Practice

**Argument**: AI development should integrate critical humanities perspectives with technical work.

**Source**: [AI interdisciplinarity as critical technical practice](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-forum-on-ai-culture-and-society/article/ai-interdisciplinarity-as-critical-technical-practice/8465ABCE2EC83AC02511C05652206E49)

**Analysis**: Proposes dissolving thinking/building boundary. Building without critical thinking reproduces power structures; thinking without building remains ineffectual. Echoes Marx's praxis.

### The Philosophic Turn for AI Agents

**Argument**: AI should facilitate decentralized truth-seeking via Socratic inquiry in community ecosystems.

**Sources**:
- [The Philosophic Turn for AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18601)
- [Published version](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11299-025-00326-z)

**Relevance**: Suggests thinking emerges from community inquiry practices. Building communities of inquiry IS the way to support thinking.

### Philosophy Eats AI

**Claim**: "Philosophy is increasingly determining how digital technologies reason, predict, create, generate, and innovate"

**Source**: [Philosophy Eats AI](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/philosophy-eats-ai/)

**Analysis**: Building AI requires philosophical thinking (even if implicit). Every AI system embodies philosophical commitments. Arendt's concern realized: making dominates, but depends on unexamined philosophical choices.

## VI. Engineering Epistemology & Practice

### Engineering Between Theory and Practice

**Argument**: Engineering requires autonomous epistemic practices—cannot be reduced to applied science.

**Source**: [Engineering Epistemology: Between Theory and Practice](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19378629.2022.2124025)

**Implications**:
- AI development is engineering, not pure science
- Building generates knowledge unobtainable through pure theorizing
- But also requires theoretical tools, not just trial-and-error

**Analysis**: Challenges both extremes—cannot understand AI without building it; cannot build safely without theoretical frameworks.

## VII. Contemporary 2026 Trends

### Pragmatic Building Over Moonshots

**Finding**: "Executives who focus on small, problem-solving wins with AI... will be the winners in 2026"

**Sources**:

**Analysis**: Pragmatic building valued over ambitious thinking. Echoes Dewey's instrumentalism. Risk: short-term building without long-term thinking?

### Critical Thinking Atrophy

**Prediction**: "Through 2026, atrophy of critical-thinking skills, due to GenAI use, will push 50% of global organizations to require 'AI-free' skills assessments"

**Source**: [Strategic Predictions for 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/strategic-predictions-for-2026)

**Analysis**: AI tools enable building outputs without thinking; thinking skills may atrophy. Relates to Polanyi: if tacit knowledge comes from practice, and AI eliminates practice, how is it maintained?

## VIII. Unresolved Debates

### The Stochastic Parrots Debate (Ongoing)

**For genuine reasoning**: Novel problem-solving, structured internal representations, performance exceeds memorization.

**For stochastic parrots**: Still pattern matching; no grounding; appearance ≠ reality. Bender maintains position (2024-2026).

**Status**: Empirically unresolved; may require resolving philosophical questions about what constitutes "genuine" reasoning.

### Dreyfus and Modern AI

**Question**: Do neural networks escape phenomenological critique?

**Status**: Active debate. Neo-Dreyfusian positions emerging.

### Theory vs. Empirical Capabilities

**Tension**: Should AI research prioritize theoretical understanding or empirical capabilities?

**Current**: Empirical far ahead of theory. Related to reproducibility crisis?
