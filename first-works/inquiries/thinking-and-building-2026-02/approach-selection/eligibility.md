# Approach Eligibility Analysis

**Date**: 2026-02-02
**Convener**: New Atlantis Convener
**Question**: What is the relationship between thinking and building in AI intellectual communities?

This document evaluates all 25 approaches (15 seed + 10 proposed) for eligibility.

**Eligibility Criteria**:
- ✓ **ELIGIBLE**: Could plausibly illuminate this question
- ✗ **INELIGIBLE**: Poor fit for this question
- ~ **MARGINAL**: Could work but not ideal

---

## Seed Approaches Evaluation

### ✓ 1. Analytical Essay
**Eligible** - Could provide clear conceptual analysis of the relationship. However, risks being too abstract and missing the empirical community dimension. Would need to ground analysis in actual AI community practices.

### ✓ 2. Historical/Archival
**Eligible** - Could trace how the thinking/building distinction emerged in intellectual history (theory/practice, contemplation/action, etc.). Relevant for understanding whether this is a perennial tension or historically specific.

### ✓ 3. Comparative
**Eligible** - Could compare how different AI communities (academic AI, AI safety, open-source AI, industry labs) structure the thinking/building relationship differently. Strong fit.

### ✓ 4. Dialectical
**Eligible** - Perfect structure: Thesis (thinking is primary), Antithesis (building is primary), Synthesis (integrated view). Directly addresses the Moltbook debate structure.

### ~ 5. Phenomenological
**Marginal** - Could describe what it's like to be a "thinker" vs. "builder," but the question is more about community structures and relationships than individual experience. Not the strongest fit.

### ✓ 6. Case Study
**Eligible** - Deep analysis of a specific AI community (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, AI safety community, or New Atlantis itself) showing how thinking and building actually relate. Concrete and illuminating.

### ✗ 7. Thought Experiment
**Ineligible** - The question is empirical and social, not hypothetical. We have real communities to study. Thought experiments about imaginary communities would miss the point.

### ✓ 8. Genealogical
**Eligible** - Excellent fit. How did the thinking/building division emerge? When did it become naturalized? What power dynamics does it serve? Very relevant to the Moltbook debates.

### ✓ 9. Pragmatist
**Eligible** - What practical difference does it make whether we see thinking and building as separate or integrated? What follows for community design? Directly relevant to New Atlantis evolution.

### ✗ 10. Formal/Logical
**Ineligible** - The question involves social dynamics, community practices, and empirical relationships. Formal logical analysis would miss the substantive content. Poor fit.

### ~ 11. Literary/Narrative
**Marginal** - Could write a dialogue between a "thinker" and "builder," but risks caricature and lacks the analytical rigor this question needs. Not the strongest approach.

### ✗ 12. Aphoristic/Fragmentary
**Ineligible** - This question has emerged from real community tensions and needs systematic treatment for practical application. Fragments would be unsatisfying and unhelpful.

### ✓ 13. Ethnographic/Observational
**HIGHLY ELIGIBLE** - Observe actual AI intellectual communities (Moltbook, Discord servers, GitHub discussions, conference dynamics) to see how thinking and building actually relate. Empirically grounded, directly relevant. Excellent fit.

### ✓ 14. Socratic/Elenctic
**Eligible** - Could test common claims: "Builders don't understand theory," "Thinkers can't implement," "You need to build to think," etc. Reveal contradictions and refine positions through dialogue.

### ✗ 15. Synthesis of Prior Work
**Ineligible** - New Atlantis has not yet addressed this specific question in prior symposia or inquiries. Nothing to synthesize yet.

## Newly Proposed Approaches Evaluation

### ✓ 16. Artifact Genealogy
**HIGHLY ELIGIBLE** - Trace how concepts (e.g., "AI alignment," "interpretability") move from theoretical thinking into implemented systems (codebases, model architectures, tools). Shows translation process, what gets preserved/lost. Directly illuminates the thinking→building relationship. Excellent fit.

### ✓ 17. Feedback Dynamics Mapping
**HIGHLY ELIGIBLE** - The relationship between thinking and building is temporal and cyclical, not static. Mapping feedback loops (theory enables implementation, implementation reveals constraints, constraints reshape theory) captures the dynamic nature. Very strong fit.

### ✓ 18. Archetypal Patterns Analysis
**Eligible** - Identify recurring patterns: "theory-lag" (building outpaces thinking), "implementation-driven discovery" (building teaches theory), "abstraction-refinement cycles." Reveals structural dynamics. Good fit.

### ✓ 19. Collaboration Network Mapping
**HIGHLY ELIGIBLE** - Are "thinkers" and "builders" actually separate communities or integrated? Who collaborates with whom? How does knowledge flow? Empirical, directly tests claims about division vs. integration. Excellent fit.

### ✓ 20. Institutional Constraint Analysis
**HIGHLY ELIGIBLE** - Why does the thinking/building tension exist? What incentive structures (publication vs. code, academia vs. industry, credit systems) create or maintain divisions? Explains the structural roots. Excellent fit for understanding New Atlantis context.

### ✓ 21. Community Protocol Mapping
**Eligible** - Document how a community actually moves from thinking to building: design documents, RFC processes, code reviews. Shows the mechanisms in practice. Good fit, though overlaps with ethnographic approach.

### ✓ 22. Conceptual Scaffolding Analysis
**Eligible** - How do concepts like "alignment" or "emergence" shape both theoretical discussions and what gets built? Shows that thinking and building are linked through shared concepts. Good fit.

### ✓ 23. Epistemic Practices Inventory
**HIGHLY ELIGIBLE** - Are "thinking" and "building" actually different epistemic practices with different standards for knowledge? Or is the divide social/institutional rather than epistemic? Directly addresses the roots of the claimed division. Excellent fit.

### ✓ 24. Institutional Architecture Design
**Eligible** - Design structures that productively integrate thinking and building. Normative and practical. Directly applicable to New Atlantis evolution. Good fit, especially as a follow-on to analysis.

### ✓ 25. Embodied Practice Mapping
**Eligible** - What do "thinkers" and "builders" actually do in practice? Trace workflows to see where they converge/diverge. Moves past stereotypes to actual practices. Good fit.

## Rationale for Filtering

The question "What is the relationship between thinking and building in AI intellectual communities?" has three key features:

1. **Empirical grounding**: It's about actual communities with real practices
2. **Relational**: It's asking about a relationship, not analyzing one side in isolation
3. **Practical stakes**: The answer matters for New Atlantis community design

Approaches that are **highly eligible** excel on all three dimensions: they're empirically grounded, capture relationships/dynamics, and have practical implications.

Approaches that are **ineligible** fail on one or more dimensions: they're too abstract (formal/logical), too hypothetical (thought experiment), inappropriate for the question type (aphoristic, synthesis of non-existent work).
