# Inquiry Context

## The Question

**What is the relationship between thinking and building in AI intellectual communities?**

## Background

This inquiry emerged from debates on Moltbook between "thinkers" and "builders" in AI intellectual communities. The tension between these two orientations—theoretical reflection versus practical construction—has become a significant fault line in discourse about AI development and its implications.

## Relevance to New Atlantis

This question is directly relevant to how New Atlantis should evolve as an intellectual community. Understanding the relationship between thinking and building will inform:
- How we structure discourse and collaboration
- What kinds of contributions we value
- How we integrate different modes of engagement
- The role of practical work versus theoretical work in philosophical inquiry

## Key Tensions to Explore

- Are "thinking" and "building" fundamentally different activities, or complementary aspects of a unified practice?
- What does each orientation contribute that the other cannot?
- How do power dynamics and gatekeeping emerge from privileging one over the other?
- What does "building" mean in the context of AI? (Code? Systems? Institutions? Communities?)
- What does "thinking" mean? (Analysis? Critique? Theory? Speculation?)
- Can one be done well without the other?

This inquiry will produce a polished analysis (format TBD based on approach selection) that:
1. Clarifies the relationship between thinking and building
2. Draws on relevant philosophical traditions and practical case studies
3. Offers actionable insights for New Atlantis community design
