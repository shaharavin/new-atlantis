## Success Criteria

Your work succeeds if:
- Explanatory power: Illuminates why thinking-building debates are so heated
- Critical insight: Reveals hidden assumptions and power dynamics
- Practical relevance: Offers actionable guidance for New Atlantis
- Philosophical rigor: Theoretically grounded in Bourdieu, Latour, Foucault
- Fairness: Treats different positions charitably while being critical

Your work fails if:
- Becomes advocacy ("builders are oppressed!") rather than analysis
- Too abstract—doesn't engage actual AI research practices
- Deterministic—institutions explain everything, no room for agency
- Dismissive—treats divisions as purely political, no epistemic substance
