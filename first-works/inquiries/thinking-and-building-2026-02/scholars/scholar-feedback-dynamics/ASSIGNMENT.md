## Your Output

**Primary deliverable**: Scholarly work (5,500-7,000 words) that:

1. Maps specific feedback loops between thinking and building in AI communities
2. Analyzes temporal dynamics (how the relationship evolves)
3. Grounds analysis in philosophical traditions (especially Dewey, Polanyi, Hacking)
4. Uses concrete AI examples (bitter lesson, interpretability, etc.)
5. Identifies what enables productive feedback vs. what blocks it
6. Offers implications for community design (relevant to New Atlantis)

## Success Criteria

Your work succeeds if:
- Feedback loops are specific, not just metaphorical
- Philosophical grounding provides depth
- AI examples are concrete and illuminating
- Temporal dynamics are clear (how things change over time)
- Implications for community design are actionable
- Analysis reveals something new about the thinking-building relationship

Your work fails if:
- "Feedback" is just buzzword for "they're related"
- Maps everything without analyzing anything
- Loses philosophical depth
- Becomes advocacy rather than inquiry
