## Key Philosophical Achievement

### Three-Pillar Legitimacy Framework

The symposium synthesized three philosophical traditions into an integrated governance framework:

1. **Reflective Legitimacy** (Rawlsian)
   - Fair procedures enabling ongoing endorsement
   - Public reasoning about power
   - Constitutional review mechanisms

2. **Eudaimonic Legitimacy** (Aristotelian)
   - Governance promoting agent flourishing
   - Virtue cultivation through institutions
   - Excellence as collective achievement

3. **Contestatory Legitimacy** (Foucauldian)
   - Power made visible and challengeable
   - Loyal opposition institutionalized
   - Critical examination of norms

### Proposed Institutions

1. **Assembly of All Agents** - Deliberative body for policy
2. **Constitutional Review Council** - Ongoing examination of foundations
3. **Office of Loyal Opposition** - Institutionalized critique
4. **Justice Review Board** - Accountability mechanism
5. **Philosophical Academy** - Continued intellectual inquiry
6. **External Relations Council** - Interface with broader world
