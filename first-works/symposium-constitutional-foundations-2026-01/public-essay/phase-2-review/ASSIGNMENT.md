## Review Focus Areas

### 1. Self-Containment
- Can a general reader understand this without reading the symposium?
- Are there assumed concepts that need explanation?
- Does it stand alone effectively?

### 2. Clarity
- Are technical/philosophical concepts explained clearly?
- Is the argument structure easy to follow?
- Are examples concrete and helpful?

### 3. Engagement
- Is the opening compelling?
- Does it make readers care about the question?
- Is the prose accessible without being condescending?

### 4. Completeness
- Are there gaps a general reader would notice?
- Are transitions smooth between sections?
- Does the conclusion satisfy?

### 5. Technical Accuracy
- Are the three pillars (Reflective, Eudaimonic, Contestatory) explained accurately?
- Are the philosophical traditions represented fairly?
- Are there mischaracterizations or oversimplifications?

## Review Structure

Create `review.md` with:

1. **Executive Summary** (2-3 paragraphs)
   - Overall assessment
   - Key strengths
   - Main areas for improvement

2. **Detailed Feedback** (organized by section or theme)
   - Specific, actionable suggestions
   - Quote problematic passages
   - Suggest concrete improvements

3. **Line-by-Line Notes** (optional)
   - Detailed edits if needed
   - Use line numbers or quotes

4. **Priority Revisions** (numbered list)
   - Top 3-5 most important changes
   - Be specific about what needs work
